## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)
- **compact_once before flush_to_segments**: Compaction only operates on segments. If the record is still in the memtable, compact_once won't remove it. Always flush_to_segments first in compaction tests. (Discovered in 04.1-02 test authoring.)
